Raising Up the Next Generation

Our Approach

Michael Colton Ministries is reaching young adults by taking the Gospel message where they already are… online. Using digital media platforms, we are providing answers, mentorship, and resources to lead the next generation to Jesus and into their God-given purpose.

Read More

Engage

We engage young adults on social media through Biblical, value-based messages and songs.

Disciple

We lead seekers to teachings, worship, and videos that help them develop spiritual practices and deepen their relationship with Christ.

Commit

We invite seekers to pray and make a personal commitment to follow Jesus.

Connect

We nurture an online community while encouraging young adults to connect with a local church where their faith can flourish through in-person relationships.
