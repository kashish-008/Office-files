MISSION

We exist to reach, mentor, and launch young adult seekers into a relationship with Christ and their God-given purpose through online platforms, digital marketing, and social media.

VISION

Our five-year vision will offer salvation and mentoring to the over one-million young adults by the end of 2030.

COMMUNITY

We are committed to fostering a Christ-centered online community while encouraging young adults to engage in a local church where their faith can flourish in a physical community.

To request a copy of our Ministry Brief, which outlines our vision, opportunities, and current needs, please email info@michaelcolton.net.
