FAQs

Every gift helps Michael Colton Ministries share the Gospel and mentor the next generation. Your generosity enables us to introduce young adults to Jesus Christ, strengthen their faith, and equip them to discover and live out their God-given purpose. To learn more about our strategy, visit our About page.

There are many ways to partner with Michael Colton Ministries and help reach the next generation with the hope of Jesus Christ. Whether you choose to give online, donate appreciated assets, make a planned gift, or become a monthly partner, every contribution helps advance our mission. Explore the giving options at the top of this page to find the method that's right for you.

Michael Colton Ministries follows the financial accountability standards established by the Evangelical Council for Financial Accountability (ECFA), which require that at least 78% of ministry revenue be directed toward program activities. Historically, we have invested more than 80% directly into program expenses.

Setting up recurring giving is simple. When making your online donation, select Monthly under the donation frequency, and your gift will automatically continue each month until you choose to cancel. If you would prefer assistance, call 800-345-5850 (Option 1), and a member of our team will be happy to help.

If your payment method, mailing address, email address, or other account information has changed, we're happy to assist. 800-345-5850 (Option 3) or email info@michaelcolton.net and our team will help you update your information.

For donors within the United States, contributions to Michael Colton Ministries are tax-deductible to the fullest extent permitted by law. An annual giving statement will be emailed for all donations made during the calendar year. If you donate non-cash assets or gifts in kind, a receipt will generally be issued within 10 days after the gift is received. For donors outside the United States, tax benefits vary by country. Please consult your local tax advisor or review your country's charitable giving regulations to determine whether your gift qualifies for a tax deduction or credit.
